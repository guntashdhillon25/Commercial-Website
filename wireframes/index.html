Build notes

1

Global header on every page. Phone number stays visible; "Get a Valuation" is the one persistent primary button. Collapses to a menu under 720px.

2

Hero: your own photo and a first‑person promise, with a button for buyers and one for sellers. People hire a person, so lead with your face.

3

Quick search — use the myRealPage listing search widget, or plain links into filtered listing pages if business listings aren't on MLS.

4

Featured listings: 3 cards, never the business name — industry + city only, to protect confidentiality.

5

Your numbers, not the brokerage’s. Only include figures you can back up; drop this band if you’re just starting out.

6

Audience split — two short paths into Sell (06) and Buyer Guide (07).

7

Testimonials, attributed by industry rather than name.

8

Closing CTA band, reused on 06, 07 and 08.

9

Global footer: your contact details, plus your brokerage name (and often its logo). Most licensing rules require this on a personal agent site.
