Office space ranges from small suites to full floors. Medical offices with plumbing already in place are included.

Investment properties already have tenants paying rent, so income starts on day one.

Lease or buy?

Most businesses start by leasing: it needs less cash upfront and makes it easier to move. Buying makes sense when you plan to stay a long time and can put down 25–35%. See should your business lease or buy.
