How to lease commercial space: a step-by-step guide

The steps from working out what you need to getting the keys, with typical timelines for each.

7 min read · Updated September 2026 · HomeQuest Commercial

Start 6 to 12 months ahead

Finding a space, negotiating and getting permits usually takes longer than people expect. Most tenants need 4 to 10 weeks to find and agree on a space, then more time for permits and build-out. Restaurants and medical offices often take longer.

1. Work out what you need

Write down the size range, the areas that work for your customers and staff, your total monthly budget, and anything the space must have, such as parking, loading doors or a kitchen. If you aren’t sure what type of property you need, our guided search can help.

2. Compare total cost, not just rent

Landlords quote rent per square foot per year, plus additional rent. A space with low base rent can cost more once additional rent is added, so compare the estimated monthly total. See what commercial space really costs.

3. View and shortlist

Visit your top 3 to 5 spaces. Check the condition, power, washrooms, parking, signage and neighbours. Ask what the previous tenant used the space for, and why they left.

4. Make an offer to lease

An offer to lease sets out the rent, the length of the lease, the start date, any free-rent period and any work the landlord will do. It usually includes conditions, such as confirming your use with the city, that give you time to check before you commit.

5. Negotiate and sign the lease

Once the main terms are agreed, the landlord’s lawyer prepares the lease. Have your own lawyer review it. Look closely at renewal options, who pays for repairs, whether you can transfer the lease if you sell your business, and any personal guarantee.

6. Permits, build-out and move-in

Most businesses need a business licence, and many need building permits for changes to the space. Ask for a rent-free fixturing period to cover the weeks you spend building out before you open.

Common questions

How long does it take to lease commercial space?

Usually 4 to 10 weeks from the first viewing to signing, plus time for permits and build-out.

Do I need a lawyer to sign a commercial lease?

It’s strongly recommended. Commercial leases are long, and they give tenants fewer protections than residential leases do.

Have a question about your situation?

An advisor can answer it, usually the same day.
