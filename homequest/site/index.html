Market

Commercial real estate in Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley

HomeQuest covers commercial real estate across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley: shops and restaurants on busy high streets, offices near SkyTrain, warehouses near the port and highways, and businesses for sale. Rents and prices generally fall the further you go from Vancouver, while newer buildings and parking become easier to find.

Most commercial space here is leased rather than bought. Rent is quoted per square foot per year, usually on a net lease, so we also show an estimated monthly total. If you’re buying, we show the down payment, property transfer tax and an estimated mortgage payment.

Industrial space is in short supply across the region, and good retail units on busy streets lease quickly. If you have a specific need, tell us early so we can watch for space before it’s advertised.

FAQ

Common questions

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

How much will a commercial lease really cost each month?

Add the base rent and the additional rent, multiply by the size in square feet, and divide by 12. Then add 5% GST. We do this for you on every listing.

Do I pay a commission to use HomeQuest?

In most leases and purchases, the landlord or seller pays the commission. We’ll tell you up front if a deal is different.

How much do I need to buy commercial property?

Plan for a down payment of about 25–35% of the price, plus property transfer tax, legal fees and inspections. See financing for details.

How long does it take to lease a space?

Usually 4 to 10 weeks from the first viewing to signing. Restaurants and medical offices can take longer because of permits.

Which areas does HomeQuest cover?

Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, including Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, Langley, Richmond, Abbotsford, Delta, Coquitlam and New Westminster.

What happens after I send a question?

An advisor replies within one business day, usually the same day. You won’t be added to a mailing list unless you ask.
