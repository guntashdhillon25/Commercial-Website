What commercial space really costs each month

How base rent, additional rent and GST add up, with a worked example.

5 min read · Updated September 2026 · HomeQuest Commercial

Rent is quoted per square foot per year

Commercial rent is almost always quoted in dollars per square foot per year. A “$32” space doesn’t cost $32 a month; it costs $32 for every square foot, every year.

Base rent plus additional rent

Most leases are net leases: you pay base rent for the space, plus additional rent for your share of property taxes, insurance and building upkeep. Additional rent is an estimate, adjusted each year to match actual costs.

A worked example

1,450 sf at $32 base rent and $11.50 additional rent:

  • $32 + $11.50 = $43.50 per square foot per year
  • $43.50 × 1,450 sf = $63,075 a year
  • $63,075 ÷ 12 = $5,256 a month
  • Plus 5% GST = about $5,519 a month

Upfront costs

Expect a deposit of about 2 to 3 months’ rent when you sign, plus your build-out, furniture and equipment, permits and legal fees. Ask whether the landlord will offer a tenant improvement allowance or free-rent months, especially on a longer lease.

Costs that change

Additional rent goes up and down with taxes and building costs, and base rent usually steps up every year or two. Ask for last year’s actual additional rent and the rent schedule for the full term, so you can budget beyond the first year. Then compare spaces for lease on their monthly totals.

Common questions

Is GST charged on commercial rent?

Yes. GST applies to commercial rent in Canada. GST-registered businesses can usually claim it back.

What does additional rent include?

Usually property taxes, building insurance and the upkeep of shared areas such as parking lots, landscaping, snow removal and the roof. Your lease lists exactly what is included.
