Buying in Burnaby

Retail property for sale in Burnaby: what to know

Burnaby’s growth is centred on four town centres along SkyTrain: Metrotown, Brentwood, Lougheed and Edmonds, each with new office towers and shopping. South Burnaby and the Still Creek area have established industrial and business parks close to Vancouver.

Retail space is for businesses that serve customers in person: shops, salons, clinics, services, showrooms and takeout. Visibility, parking and the businesses next door matter as much as the space itself.

Where to look in Burnaby

MetrotownOne of the region’s largest shopping centres, with office towers and Expo Line stations.
BrentwoodA fast-growing town centre on the Millennium Line, with new retail and office space.
LougheedA town centre where the Millennium Line meets the Lougheed Highway.
EdmondsNeighbourhood shopping and services in southeast Burnaby.
Big BendIndustrial and business park land along the Fraser River in south Burnaby.
Still CreekBusiness park and light industrial space near Highway 1 and the Vancouver border.

Getting around: Highway 1 and the Lougheed Highway, and the SkyTrain Expo and Millennium lines.

What to check before you buy

  • Your use is allowed under the zoning
  • Signage: where your sign can go and how big it can be
  • Parking for your customers, and whether it is shared
  • Exclusive-use clauses that stop a competitor opening next door
  • Power, plumbing and ventilation for your equipment
  • The total monthly cost, including additional rent

FAQ

Questions about retail property for sale in Burnaby

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Is Burnaby good for office space near transit?

Yes. Metrotown, Brentwood and Lougheed all have office buildings a short walk from SkyTrain, often at lower rents than downtown Vancouver.

Does Burnaby have industrial space for lease?

Yes, mainly in Big Bend along the Fraser River and around Still Creek. Vacancy is usually low, so good units lease quickly.

How much does retail space cost to lease?

Retail rent is quoted per square foot per year, plus additional rent for taxes and upkeep. Add both, multiply by the size and divide by 12 for a monthly figure. Every listing here shows that estimate.

What is an inline retail unit?

A unit in the middle of a row of shops, as opposed to an end unit. End units usually have more visibility and sometimes room for a drive-thru, and they cost more.

How much down payment do I need for commercial property?

Lenders usually ask for 25–35% of the price. Businesses buying their own premises may qualify for less through programs such as BDC.

What costs are there on top of the price?

Plan for property transfer tax (1% of the first $200,000 and 2% of the rest for commercial property), 5% GST unless you are GST-registered, legal fees, inspections and an appraisal.

How long does it take to buy a commercial property?

Usually 60 to 120 days from accepted offer to completion, including 30 to 60 days of due diligence.
