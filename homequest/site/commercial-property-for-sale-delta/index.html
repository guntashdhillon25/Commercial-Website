Buying in Delta

Commercial property for sale in Delta: what to know

Delta has large industrial areas at Tilbury and Annacis Island, close to the Deltaport container terminal and the main highways. Its retail mainly serves the neighbourhoods: North Delta along Scott Road, and the villages of Ladner and Tsawwassen.

Where to look in Delta

TilburyA major industrial area with modern warehouses near Highway 17 and Highway 99.
Annacis IslandAn industrial island in the Fraser River with manufacturing and distribution.
North DeltaShopping along Scott Road and 120 Street, next to Surrey.
LadnerA village centre with independent shops and services.
TsawwassenTsawwassen Mills shopping centre and the BC Ferries terminal.

Getting around: Highway 17, Highway 91 and Highway 99, close to Deltaport and the Tsawwassen ferry terminal.

FAQ

Questions about commercial property for sale in Delta

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Why do logistics companies choose Delta?

It’s close to Deltaport and the main highways, so trucks can reach the port, the airport and the US border quickly.

Is there retail space in Delta?

Yes, mostly neighbourhood plazas in North Delta, Ladner and Tsawwassen. These suit services, food and everyday retail.

How much down payment do I need for commercial property?

Lenders usually ask for 25–35% of the price. Businesses buying their own premises may qualify for less through programs such as BDC.

What costs are there on top of the price?

Plan for property transfer tax (1% of the first $200,000 and 2% of the rest for commercial property), 5% GST unless you are GST-registered, legal fees, inspections and an appraisal.

How long does it take to buy a commercial property?

Usually 60 to 120 days from accepted offer to completion, including 30 to 60 days of due diligence.
