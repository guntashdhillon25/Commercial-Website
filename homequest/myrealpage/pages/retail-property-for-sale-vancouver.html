Buying in Vancouver

Retail property for sale in Vancouver: what to know

Vancouver’s commercial space is spread along its high streets, such as Main Street, Commercial Drive and West 4th Avenue, as well as downtown. Space is tighter and rents are higher than in the suburbs, but foot traffic and transit access are hard to match. Industrial space is scarce and concentrated near False Creek Flats and the Fraser River.

Retail space is for businesses that serve customers in person: shops, salons, clinics, services, showrooms and takeout. Visibility, parking and the businesses next door matter as much as the space itself.

Where to look in Vancouver

DowntownThe region’s main office district, plus Robson Street and other major shopping streets.
Mount PleasantMain Street and Broadway: independent shops, restaurants and creative offices, with the Broadway Subway under construction.
KitsilanoWest 4th Avenue and West Broadway, with strong neighbourhood shopping and dining.
Commercial DriveA long-established street of cafés, restaurants and independent shops.
False Creek FlatsLight industrial space, studios, breweries and tech offices east of Main Street.

Getting around: SkyTrain Expo, Millennium and Canada lines, and frequent buses on the main streets.

What to check before you buy

  • Your use is allowed under the zoning
  • Signage: where your sign can go and how big it can be
  • Parking for your customers, and whether it is shared
  • Exclusive-use clauses that stop a competitor opening next door
  • Power, plumbing and ventilation for your equipment
  • The total monthly cost, including additional rent

FAQ

Questions about retail property for sale in Vancouver

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Why are commercial rents higher in Vancouver?

There is little room for new commercial space and demand is high, especially on busy streets near transit. Many tenants accept higher rent in exchange for more foot traffic and a better-known location.

Can I find restaurant space with a kitchen in Vancouver?

Yes. Spaces with a kitchen already built come up often, especially on Main Street, Commercial Drive and in Kitsilano. They tend to lease quickly, so it helps to have an advisor watching for them.

How much does retail space cost to lease?

Retail rent is quoted per square foot per year, plus additional rent for taxes and upkeep. Add both, multiply by the size and divide by 12 for a monthly figure. Every listing here shows that estimate.

What is an inline retail unit?

A unit in the middle of a row of shops, as opposed to an end unit. End units usually have more visibility and sometimes room for a drive-thru, and they cost more.

How much down payment do I need for commercial property?

Lenders usually ask for 25–35% of the price. Businesses buying their own premises may qualify for less through programs such as BDC.

What costs are there on top of the price?

Plan for property transfer tax (1% of the first $200,000 and 2% of the rest for commercial property), 5% GST unless you are GST-registered, legal fees, inspections and an appraisal.

How long does it take to buy a commercial property?

Usually 60 to 120 days from accepted offer to completion, including 30 to 60 days of due diligence.
