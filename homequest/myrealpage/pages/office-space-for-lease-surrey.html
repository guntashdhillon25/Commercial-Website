Leasing in Surrey

Office space for lease in Surrey: what to know

Surrey has several town centres rather than one downtown, so the right area depends on who your customers are. Retail and services follow the new housing in Fleetwood, Clayton and South Surrey. Offices cluster around City Centre and its SkyTrain stations, and Campbell Heights is one of the region’s main industrial parks.

Office space ranges from small suites for a few people to full floors for large teams, plus medical and dental offices with plumbing already installed. Transit access and parking often matter most to staff and clients.

What to check before you lease

  • How many people it seats, and room to grow
  • Parking and transit for staff and clients
  • Building hours and after-hours access
  • Who pays for improvements such as walls and wiring
  • Whether furniture is included
  • For medical use: plumbing, accessibility and zoning for clinics

FAQ

Questions about office space for lease in Surrey

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Which part of Surrey is best for a retail business?

It depends on your customers. Newton and Guildford have established shopping traffic. Fleetwood, Clayton and South Surrey have newer plazas near fast-growing neighbourhoods, with more parking and newer buildings.

Is Surrey a good place for warehouse space?

Yes. Campbell Heights, Cloverdale and Port Kells have large, modern industrial buildings with good truck access to the highways and the US border.

How much office space do I need?

A common rule of thumb is 150 to 250 square feet per person, including meeting rooms and a kitchen. Open-plan offices need less; offices with many private rooms need more.

Can I buy an office instead of leasing one?

Yes. A strata office lets a business own its own unit, often with a mortgage payment close to what it would pay in rent. You’ll usually need a down payment of 25–35%.

How much does it cost to lease commercial space?

Commercial rent is quoted per square foot per year, in two parts: base rent and additional rent (taxes, insurance and upkeep). Add them, multiply by the size and divide by 12, then add 5% GST. We show this monthly estimate on every listing.

How long is a typical commercial lease?

Most retail and office leases run 3 to 10 years, often with an option to renew. Restaurants usually sign for 10 years or more.

Do tenants pay a commission?

In most commercial leases, the landlord pays the leasing commission. We’ll confirm how it works before you commit.
