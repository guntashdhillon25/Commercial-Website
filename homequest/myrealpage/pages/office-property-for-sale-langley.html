Buying in Langley

Office property for sale in Langley: what to know

Langley is two separate municipalities: the City of Langley, around the downtown core, and the much larger Township of Langley, which includes Willoughby, Walnut Grove and the Gloucester industrial area. Zoning and permits depend on which one a property is in, so check this early.

Office space ranges from small suites for a few people to full floors for large teams, plus medical and dental offices with plumbing already installed. Transit access and parking often matter most to staff and clients.

What to check before you buy

  • How many people it seats, and room to grow
  • Parking and transit for staff and clients
  • Building hours and after-hours access
  • Who pays for improvements such as walls and wiring
  • Whether furniture is included
  • For medical use: plumbing, accessibility and zoning for clinics

FAQ

Questions about office property for sale in Langley

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

What’s the difference between the City and the Township of Langley?

They are separate municipalities with their own zoning, permits and property taxes. The City of Langley is the smaller downtown area. The Township covers everything else, including Willoughby, Walnut Grove and Gloucester.

Is Langley good for warehouse space?

Yes. Gloucester and the areas along Highway 1 have large modern buildings with dock loading, often at lower rents than in Burnaby or Richmond.

How much office space do I need?

A common rule of thumb is 150 to 250 square feet per person, including meeting rooms and a kitchen. Open-plan offices need less; offices with many private rooms need more.

Can I buy an office instead of leasing one?

Yes. A strata office lets a business own its own unit, often with a mortgage payment close to what it would pay in rent. You’ll usually need a down payment of 25–35%.

How much down payment do I need for commercial property?

Lenders usually ask for 25–35% of the price. Businesses buying their own premises may qualify for less through programs such as BDC.

What costs are there on top of the price?

Plan for property transfer tax (1% of the first $200,000 and 2% of the rest for commercial property), 5% GST unless you are GST-registered, legal fees, inspections and an appraisal.

How long does it take to buy a commercial property?

Usually 60 to 120 days from accepted offer to completion, including 30 to 60 days of due diligence.
