Commercial zoning in BC: what it allows and how to check

What zoning is, how to check what a property allows, and what to do if your use isn’t permitted.

5 min read · Updated September 2026 · HomeQuest Commercial

What zoning does

Zoning is each city’s set of rules for what a property can be used for and what can be built on it. It decides whether a space can be a café, a clinic, a gym or a warehouse, and sets limits on building height, parking and signs.

Every city uses its own codes

A “C-2” zone in Vancouver is different from a “C-2” zone in Surrey or Burnaby. Always read the zoning bylaw for the city where the property is, or ask us to check. In Langley, check whether the property is in the City or the Township, as they have separate bylaws.

Common zone types

  • Commercial: shops, offices, restaurants and services
  • Industrial: warehousing, manufacturing, repair and trades, with limited retail
  • Mixed-use: shops at street level with homes or offices above
  • Comprehensive development (CD): a custom zone written for one site, with its own list of allowed uses

How to check before you sign

Look up the property’s zoning on the city’s online map, then find your use in the bylaw. Some uses need extra approvals even when the zone allows them, such as liquor licences, daycares or cannabis stores. Make your offer subject to confirming your use with the city.

If your use isn’t allowed

You can apply for a rezoning or a temporary use permit, but it can take a year or more and isn’t guaranteed. In most cases it’s faster to find a property that already allows your use.

Common questions

Can I open a gym in a commercial zone?

Often, but not always. Some zones list fitness studios as a permitted use and some don’t, and parking rules can be an issue. Check the bylaw for the specific property.
