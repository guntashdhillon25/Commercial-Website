Buying in Surrey

Businesses for sale in Surrey: what to know

Surrey has several town centres rather than one downtown, so the right area depends on who your customers are. Retail and services follow the new housing in Fleetwood, Clayton and South Surrey. Offices cluster around City Centre and its SkyTrain stations, and Campbell Heights is one of the region’s main industrial parks.

Buying a business means taking over a working operation: customers, staff, equipment, suppliers and usually the lease. Most small businesses are priced on what they earn for their owner each year.

Where to look in Surrey

City CentreTowers, offices and SkyTrain access around King George Boulevard. Suits professional offices and services.
NewtonEstablished retail along King George Boulevard and 72 Avenue, with a large, diverse customer base.
FleetwoodNeighbourhood plazas along Fraser Highway, where the SkyTrain extension to Langley is being built.
GuildfordRegional shopping around Guildford Town Centre, close to Highway 1.
CloverdaleHistoric town centre, plus light industrial land near Highway 10 and Highway 15.
Campbell HeightsModern industrial business park near Highway 1 and the Pacific Highway border crossing.

Getting around: Highway 1, Highway 99, Highway 10 and Highway 15, and the SkyTrain Expo Line to City Centre.

What to check before you buy

  • Owner’s cash flow (SDE), and how it was calculated
  • Three years of financial statements and tax returns
  • Time left on the lease, and whether it can be transferred to you
  • Whether key staff and customers are likely to stay
  • What’s included: equipment, inventory, brand and website
  • How much training the seller will give

FAQ

Questions about businesses for sale in Surrey

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Which part of Surrey is best for a retail business?

It depends on your customers. Newton and Guildford have established shopping traffic. Fleetwood, Clayton and South Surrey have newer plazas near fast-growing neighbourhoods, with more parking and newer buildings.

Is Surrey a good place for warehouse space?

Yes. Campbell Heights, Cloverdale and Port Kells have large, modern industrial buildings with good truck access to the highways and the US border.

Why are business listings confidential?

Owners don’t want staff, customers or competitors to find out before a sale is agreed. You sign a short confidentiality agreement (NDA) to see the name, address and financial statements.

How are small businesses valued?

Usually as a multiple of the owner’s yearly cash flow (SDE), often between 1.5 and 3 times, depending on the industry, how steady the income is and how much depends on the owner.
