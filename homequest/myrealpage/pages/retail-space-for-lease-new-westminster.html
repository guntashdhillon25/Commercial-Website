Leasing in New Westminster

Retail space for lease in New Westminster: what to know

New Westminster is one of the region’s oldest cities. It is small but central, with shops and restaurants on Columbia Street, Uptown around Sixth Street, and medical offices near Royal Columbian Hospital in Sapperton. Queensborough, across the river, has industrial land and a shopping centre.

Retail space is for businesses that serve customers in person: shops, salons, clinics, services, showrooms and takeout. Visibility, parking and the businesses next door matter as much as the space itself.

What to check before you lease

  • Your use is allowed under the zoning
  • Signage: where your sign can go and how big it can be
  • Parking for your customers, and whether it is shared
  • Exclusive-use clauses that stop a competitor opening next door
  • Power, plumbing and ventilation for your equipment
  • The total monthly cost, including additional rent

FAQ

Questions about retail space for lease in New Westminster

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Is New Westminster good for a medical office?

Yes, especially in Sapperton near Royal Columbian Hospital, where clinics and specialists cluster.

Are there heritage buildings for sale in New Westminster?

Downtown has many older buildings with shops on the ground floor and apartments or offices above. Check for heritage status, which can limit changes to the outside of the building.

How much does retail space cost to lease?

Retail rent is quoted per square foot per year, plus additional rent for taxes and upkeep. Add both, multiply by the size and divide by 12 for a monthly figure. Every listing here shows that estimate.

What is an inline retail unit?

A unit in the middle of a row of shops, as opposed to an end unit. End units usually have more visibility and sometimes room for a drive-thru, and they cost more.

How much does it cost to lease commercial space?

Commercial rent is quoted per square foot per year, in two parts: base rent and additional rent (taxes, insurance and upkeep). Add them, multiply by the size and divide by 12, then add 5% GST. We show this monthly estimate on every listing.

How long is a typical commercial lease?

Most retail and office leases run 3 to 10 years, often with an option to renew. Restaurants usually sign for 10 years or more.

Do tenants pay a commission?

In most commercial leases, the landlord pays the leasing commission. We’ll confirm how it works before you commit.
