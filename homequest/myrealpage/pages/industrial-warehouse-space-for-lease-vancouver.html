Leasing in Vancouver

Industrial and warehouse space for lease in Vancouver: what to know

Vancouver’s commercial space is spread along its high streets, such as Main Street, Commercial Drive and West 4th Avenue, as well as downtown. Space is tighter and rents are higher than in the suburbs, but foot traffic and transit access are hard to match. Industrial space is scarce and concentrated near False Creek Flats and the Fraser River.

Industrial space is for businesses that store, make, fix or ship things. Clear height, loading doors, power and yard space usually decide whether a building works before price does.

Getting around: SkyTrain Expo, Millennium and Canada lines, and frequent buses on the main streets.

What to check before you lease

  • Clear height for your racking or equipment
  • Dock-level and drive-in doors for your trucks
  • Power supply (amps and voltage) for your machines
  • Sprinklers and floor strength for what you store
  • Yard space for trucks and trailers
  • Zoning for your specific activity

FAQ

Questions about industrial and warehouse space for lease in Vancouver

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

Why are commercial rents higher in Vancouver?

There is little room for new commercial space and demand is high, especially on busy streets near transit. Many tenants accept higher rent in exchange for more foot traffic and a better-known location.

Can I find restaurant space with a kitchen in Vancouver?

Yes. Spaces with a kitchen already built come up often, especially on Main Street, Commercial Drive and in Kitsilano. They tend to lease quickly, so it helps to have an advisor watching for them.

What is the difference between dock and grade loading?

A dock door is at truck-bed height, so pallets roll straight on and off a semi-trailer. A grade-level (drive-in) door is at ground level, so vans and smaller trucks can drive inside.

Why is industrial space hard to find in Metro Vancouver?

Mountains, water, farmland and the US border limit how much land can be built on, so industrial vacancy is usually low. It helps to start looking 6 to 12 months ahead.

How much does it cost to lease commercial space?

Commercial rent is quoted per square foot per year, in two parts: base rent and additional rent (taxes, insurance and upkeep). Add them, multiply by the size and divide by 12, then add 5% GST. We show this monthly estimate on every listing.

How long is a typical commercial lease?

Most retail and office leases run 3 to 10 years, often with an option to renew. Restaurants usually sign for 10 years or more.

Do tenants pay a commission?

In most commercial leases, the landlord pays the leasing commission. We’ll confirm how it works before you commit.
