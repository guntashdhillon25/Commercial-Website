# SEO audit

Built 141 pages (129 landing pages, 7 guides, glossary, help, contact, home).
Average 743 words of content per page; the shortest is /contact/ with 83.

## Problems

None: every page has a unique title (60 characters or fewer) and description (70–160), exactly one H1, and all internal links resolve.

## Fill in before launch (build/site.config.json)

- domain
- phone
- email
- brokerage
- address
- listingSearchUrl (your myRealPage listing search page)
- ogImage (optional, a 1200×630 image for link previews)
